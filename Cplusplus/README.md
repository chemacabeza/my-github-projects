## Introduction

Welcome to the **C++ Mastery curriculum**. This sequence of modules guides you from the foundations of procedural programming through the depths of memory management, advanced Object-Oriented paradigms, template metaprogramming, and down to the raw assembly and reversing architecture of compiled binaries.

The chapters are specifically designed to strip away the abstractions, giving you a deep-dive understanding of how C++ behaves "under the hood."
