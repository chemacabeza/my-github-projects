# Section 1: Introduction

## Course Overview

Welcome to **Realistic AI Images with Stable Diffusion & Fooocus**!

In this course, we will explore the powerful capabilities of **Fooocus**, an advanced image generation software based on Stable Diffusion. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced creator, this course will guide you through the process of generating stunning, photorealistic AI images.

### What You Will Learn
*   **Fooocus & Google Colab**: accessing and running the software.
*   **Image Generation**: creating high-quality images from text prompts.
*   **Image Restoration**: fixing common AI errors, such as deformed hands or artifacts.
*   **Professional Applications**: how to use these skills for commercial projects (e.g., fashion modeling).

## Tools We Will Use

### Google Colab
We will primarily use [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/) to run Fooocus. This allows us to leverage powerful cloud computing resources without needing a high-end local GPU.

## Real-World Application Preview

Throughout the course, the instructor shares insights from real-world projects. For example, using Fooocus to create digital models for fashion clients on platforms like Fiverr. You will see practical examples of how to take a simple photo of a dress and generate a realistic model wearing it.

## Addressing Common Issues

AI image generation isn't always perfect. A key part of this course focuses on **troubleshooting and refining** images. You will learn techniques to correct common issues, such as "broken hands" or unnatural anomalies, ensuring your final results are polished and professional.
