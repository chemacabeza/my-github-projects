# Section 11: Freelancing with AI

Mastering AI image generation opens up career opportunities. Platforms like **Fiverr** or **Upwork** are great places to start.

## 1. Choosing Your Platform

*   [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/): Good for specific, packaged services (Gigs). Example: "I will create a realistic fashion model for your clothing brand."
*   [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/): Good for long-term projects and hourly work.

## 2. Strategies for Success

### Finding Your Niche
Don't just offer "AI Images". Be specific:
*   *Digital Fashion Models for Clothing Brands*
*   *Realistic Portraits for Book Covers*
*   *Concept Art for Games*
*   *Consistent Influencer Creation*

### The "Golden Rules" of Freelancing
1.  **Portfolio is King**: Clients judge you by your results. Create a stunning portfolio *before* you get your first client.
2.  **Get First Reviews Fast**: Offer a competitive (low) price initially to get your first 5-star reviews. Then raise your prices.
3.  **Communication**: Be professional, polite, and clear.
4.  **Define Requirements**: Never start working without a clear agreement on what is needed (reference images, style guide, deliverables).
5.  **Know Your Worth**: Don't do endless free trials.

## 3. Creating a Gig (Fiverr)

When creating your offer:
*   **Title**: Clear and searchable (e.g., "I will create a consistent AI influencer for your brand").
*   **Images**: Use your BEST work as the gig cover. Show "Before/After" if offering editing services.
*   **Description**: Explain your process. "I use Stable Diffusion and advanced inpainting to ensure high-quality, glitch-free results."
