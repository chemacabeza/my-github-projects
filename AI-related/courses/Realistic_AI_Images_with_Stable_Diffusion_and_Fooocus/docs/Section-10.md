# Section 10: Bonus - First Steps into AI Video

Static images are great, but AI Video is the next frontier. We will use **Kling AI** to bring our influencer to life.

## Why Kling AI?
Kling AI is currently one of the best "Image-to-Video" generators (similar to Runway Gen-3 or Luma Dream Machine) offering high realism and coherent motion.

## The Workflow

1.  **Access**: Go to [Kling AI](https://klingai.com) and navigate to **AI Videos** -> **Image to Video**.
2.  **Upload**: Select your high-quality influencer image.
    *   *Tip: Use an image where movement is natural (e.g., dancing, walking, looking at camera).*

<p align="center">
    <img src="../images/section7/French-Girl-Blue-Eyes-Fixed-Blonde-2.jpeg" width="300" title="Source Image"/>
</p>

3.  **Prompt**: Describe the movement you want.
    > `Excited french girl dancing in front of the camera, smiling, high quality, 4k`
4.  **Negative Prompt**:
    > `blur, distortion, disfigurement, low quality, grainy, abstract, morphing`
5.  **Settings**:
    *   **Creativity**: `0.5` (Balances sticking to the image vs. adding new motion).
    *   **Duration**: `5s`.
6.  **Generate**.
