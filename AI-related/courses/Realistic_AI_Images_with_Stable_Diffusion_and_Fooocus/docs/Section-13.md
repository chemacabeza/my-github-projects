# Section 13: Ethics & Legalities in AI

With great power comes great responsibility.

## 1. Legal Aspects

*   **Copyright**: In many jurisdictions (like the US), pure AI-generated art cannot be copyrighted by the creator, as it lacks "human authorship." However, the laws are evolving rapidly.
*   **Commercial Use**:
    *   **Models**: Check the license of the Model/LoRA you use. Some are for "Non-Commercial Use Only" (Creative Commons Non-Commercial).
    *   **Likeness**: Using the likeness of a real celebrity for commercial gain without permission is illegal (Right of Publicity).
*   **Regulations**: Stay updated on the **EU AI Act** and local regulations regarding watermarking and disclosure.

## 2. Moral Responsibilities

*   **Deepfakes**: **NEVER** create non-consensual deepfakes (NCII). It is unethical, often illegal, and harms real people.
*   **Transparency**: Don't fool your audience.
    *   If you create an AI influencer, it is good practice to disclose it (e.g., using `#AI` or `#VirtualHuman` tags).
    *   Deceiving elderly or vulnerable people into believing an AI character is real for financial gain (scamming) is reprehensible.
*   **Harmful Content**: Do not use these tools to generate hate speech, violence, or illegal content.

## 3. Best Practices
1.  **Be Honest**: "Full Body Portrait of an AI generated model" is an honest caption.
2.  **Respect Licenses**: If a model creator says "Do not use for XYZ," respect it.
3.  **Positive Impact**: Use AI to create beauty, art, and entertainment, not deception or harm.
