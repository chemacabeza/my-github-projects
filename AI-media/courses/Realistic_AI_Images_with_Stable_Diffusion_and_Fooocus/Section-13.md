# Section 13: Legal and moral aspects of creating AI images

**Legal and Moral** aspects of creating AI images:
* ALWAYS obey the law. If you have doubt about something, do some research or contact legal consulting
* ALWAYS use your new skills for the good and not for the bad. Use it with responsibility
* AI is still quite new. Make sure you keep up to date regarding regulation and laws in your country
* Be very careful with deepfakes. AI generated images of real people. You need their permission to do that
* Respect the terms of use of certain models and LoRAs
* Don't fool people with an AI character. Especially older people have often times no idea about that new technology.
