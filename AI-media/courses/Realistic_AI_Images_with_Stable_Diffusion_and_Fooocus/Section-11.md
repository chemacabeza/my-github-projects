## What do I do on Fiverr and how do I earn money?

There are several pages like Fiverr:
* <a href="https://www.fiverr.com/">Fiverr</a>
    * In this website you can create Gigs
* <a href="https://www.upwork.com/">Upwork</a>

Instructor tips about freelancing:
* Ideally, find a niche
* Find a niche that you enjoy
* Create an appealing description (get inspiration from competitors)
* Stick to market-standard prices for your service
* Quickly get your first orders (initial price doesn't matter)
* Get good reviews
* Show your results
* Test multiple gigs
* Communicate clearly and directly with clients
* Clearly define the requirements for your projects together with the client
* Document the requirements (offer, document, chat)
* Delivery quality. Your product is your brand
* Know your value
* Set boundaries
* Don't allow yourself to be taken advantage of (trials, promises)
* ALWAYS remain professional and polite; never become emotional
