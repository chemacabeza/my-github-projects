# Section 12: Tips for an efficient and clean Work Process

## How to work in a fast and organized way

**Tips** for working efficient and clean
* Define all requirements from the beginning and document them
* Always keep the final result in mind (skin color, body type) to avoid rework
* Note the SEED number, prompt, and negative prompt of important images (reproducility)
* Work neatly and in an organized mannger (folder structure)
* Occasionally delete old drafts (to manage data size and maintain clarity)
