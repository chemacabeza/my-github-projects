## Learning Approach & Philosophy

### Depth Before Breadth
- **Master one area** before moving to the next
- **Example**: Complete Spring Boot mastery before starting Go
- **Benefit**: Deep expertise is more valuable than surface knowledge

### Project-Based Learning
- **Build real applications**, not toy examples
- **Production-ready code** with tests, documentation, error handling
- **Learning by doing** solidifies concepts better than passive reading

### Documentation as Learning
- **Write comprehensive docs** to clarify understanding
- **Teach others** through guides and examples
- **Future reference** for myself and others

### Continuous Iteration
- **Revisit earlier projects** with new knowledge
- **Refactor and improve** based on new patterns learned
- **Apply learnings** from advanced topics to earlier work

### Balanced Learning
- **70% Core Technologies**: Spring Boot, distributed systems, cloud-native
- **20% Adjacent Skills**: DevOps, testing, observability
- **10% Emerging Tech**: AI/ML, new languages, experimental
